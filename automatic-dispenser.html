Overview

The brief was to engineer and build a prototype that dispenses any desired liquid automatically when motion is detected in a defined area, usable without touching a button or handle. The final design incorporated a replaceable liquid supply bag, a hinging door with a cutout for access, and a level sensor so the unit could signal when it needed a refill.

What I did

  • Engineered and built a working prototype that dispenses liquid on motion detection.
  • Incorporated a replaceable liquid supply bag, a hinging access door with cutout, and a liquid-level sensor.
  • Refined the design to fit inside a shoebox footprint and to be usable by people with disabilities.
