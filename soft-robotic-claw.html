A four-armed, pulley-actuated soft gripper built to grasp non-standard, unevenly weighted objects mid-flight, doubling as landing gear on the drone it's mounted to.

Overview

The goal was a gripping mechanism that could reliably grasp objects of irregular shape and uneven weight distribution while attached to a drone in flight, without adding a separate landing-gear system. The result was a four-armed mechanism built from flexible material, with each arm driven independently by its own pulley, letting the claw both close around a load and set down as landing gear on touchdown.

FIG. 1. CAD render of the four-armed claw assembly mounted on the drone.

What I did

  • Researched existing mechanisms for grasping non-standard and unevenly weighted objects from a drone in flight.
  • Designed the four-armed flexible mechanism and its individual pulley-actuation system.
  • Used CAD to model the structure, calculate weight balancing on the drone, and design a mold for the claw structure.
  • Presented the proposed design to the project manager and team; it was selected out of numerous competing designs.
